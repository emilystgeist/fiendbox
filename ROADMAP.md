# FIENDBOX roadmap

What has been done and what might come. Plans change; nothing here is a promise.

## Done

- **0.1**: the FIEND palette, a new boot screen and ABOUT page, five power-on scenes with four new patterns, and the
  credits.
- **0.2**: the familiars: the engines' EDIT pages draw a creature, adapted from JIANT FM.
- **0.3**: the coven: HOME's stage as four familiars, one per track.
- **0.4**: BYTE and FLOAT, bytebeat and floatbeat as two more waves of the LOFI engine, and fourteen presets.

## Ideas

- **Mutation**: sounds that drift and rewrite themselves while you play, a timbre counterpart to DREAM.
- A short FIENDBOX guide: what is new, with the scenes, the familiars, the coven and the formulas.
- More scenes and patterns; more formulas for BYTE and FLOAT.
- A hosted installer and a browser emulator under FIENDBOX's own name.
- New Felucca releases merged as they come (FIENDBOX is on Felucca 1.4.1).
